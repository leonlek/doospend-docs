4. Financial calculations

4.1 Debt payoff plan (Avalanche / Snowball)

Estimated time and total interest are estimates that assume:

  • You do not use the card further during the payoff period
  • You pay on time every month (no late fees)
  • Interest stays fixed at the rate you set (not counting promotional 0% or cash-advance rates)
  • Interest is computed as simple monthly compounding (some real cards have a grace period)

Actual figures may differ from the app depending on your bank's policy and your behavior. Treat them as a guideline, not a promise.

4.2 Best Card Selector

Recommendations on which card pays off best are based on the reward rules you enter yourself; if you enter them wrongly, the result is wrong too. We are not liable for damage from using a card as the app recommends.

4.3 Scan-to-Pay / PromptPay QR

This is a "record before you pay" feature — the app reads a PromptPay QR to record the recipient, amount, and date. We do not carry out the transaction. You still need to open your own banking app and scan the QR to actually pay. Any transfer happens through the bank's systems; we are not involved and are not liable for damage from the transfer.

4.4 Book summary (who owes whom)

When a book has 2+ people, the app calculates "who owes whom": each bill can choose who it is split with (default = everyone in the book), split equally among the chosen group only, then paired into the fewest transfers (minimum transfers).

  • Transfer figures are based on the entries that everyone actually logs in the book — if someone forgets to log or logs incorrectly, the result is wrong too
  • We do not transfer money. You PromptPay / transfer to each other yourselves according to the figures the app suggests
  • Bills are split equally among the chosen participants — proportional/percentage (weighted) splits are not supported yet
  • We are not liable for damage from transfers made on the app's suggestion — check before you transfer
