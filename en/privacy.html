In short: your financial data does not leave your iPhone/iPad and your own private Apple iCloud (on Android: your device, plus any backup location you choose — see section 5). We have no server that stores your transactions, balances, or accounts. The only things "sent out" are the images/text you ask the AI to read — slips, card statements, order-history screenshots, and voice-note text (to Anthropic, via a Cloudflare Worker). AI requests carry only a "random ID" used to count quota and prevent overuse; it is not your name, email, or Apple ID and does not say who you are.

4. Third-party services we use

4.1 Anthropic Claude (via Cloudflare Worker)

When you use an AI feature, the following images or text are sent through a Cloudflare Worker (our own proxy) to the Anthropic Claude API to read the amount, merchant, and date and to suggest a category: slip images, credit-card statement images/text, order-history screenshots (e.g. Shopee, Lazada, TrueMoney), and voice transcripts. This data may contain financial details (merchant, amount, date) — we send it to the AI only when you ask. The app asks for your consent before sending anything to the AI for the first time (stating clearly what is sent and to whom), and you can turn it off or withdraw consent at any time in Settings › Your data — when it is off, the app sends nothing to the AI.

  • Requests carry a random ID (a random UUID created on your device and kept in the Keychain) in the x-device-id header, used to count monthly AI quota and prevent overuse. This ID survives deleting and reinstalling the app, but it is not your name, email, or Apple ID and cannot tell who you are. Requests also carry the tier (free/pro) and the app version.
  • Since version 1.8.2 this ID is kept in iCloud Keychain, so every device signed in to the same Apple ID uses the same ID — AI quota is therefore counted together across iPhone and iPad instead of separately. We never receive your Apple ID; only the random UUID is synced, and if you turn off iCloud Keychain the app falls back to an ID for that device only.
  • Anthropic does not receive this device ID (only our proxy uses it), and its policy states it does not use API requests to train models (Anthropic Privacy)
  • Anthropic processes requests under its Commercial Terms + privacy policy, which provide the same level of protection as described in this policy — inputs/outputs are not used to train models and are kept only temporarily for abuse monitoring, then deleted on Anthropic's schedule
  • Our Cloudflare Worker proxy does not log images or content — it records only the number of requests per device ID for quota / rate limiting

4.2 Apple iCloud + Apple StoreKit

Data in the CloudKit database is your own private database under your Apple ID. Apple stores it encrypted and does not read it. We have no access to it.

4.3 Sharing books via CKShare

When you invite others to a book, Apple's CKShare API handles permissions and syncing between the participants' iCloud accounts:

  • Invite links work only for Apple IDs the owner allows → people who were not invited cannot open the link
  • Data in a shared book lives in the owner's Apple iCloud shared database — Apple handles encryption + access control
  • We have no central server for books — when the owner stops sharing, participants lose access immediately
  • "Added by: name" on an entry uses Apple's Apple ID record name — not your email or phone number

6. Deleting data

  • Delete a single transaction: swipe it in the list
  • Delete slip photos in groups: Settings → "Image storage" → by image age
  • Delete everything on the device: delete the app (from the Home Screen) → this removes the data on the device and in your iCloud private database
  • Android: Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app → removes the data on the device · backup files in the folder you chose and the copy in Google's backup are not deleted with it — delete them yourself in that folder / Google Drive

7. Children

DooSpend is designed for adults (17+ per the App Store rating). The app keeps no separate data about children. If a child uses the app, the data stays on that child's device/iCloud as usual.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes (such as adding a new third party) will also be announced in the app.
